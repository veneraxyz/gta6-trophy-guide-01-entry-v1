    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, no difficulty trophies and no online trophies. The order below follows the official descriptions: story chapters first, side strands next, then the long grinds. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay.

    One missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears after the chapter. Do it, and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.

      About this guide

      Grand Theft Auto VI launched on 19 November 2026. This guide covers its 55 PlayStation 5 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden until earned — with the official name, description and grade of each, as the trophy trackers list them.

      Every guide is written for this entry in its own words. Each one cites the published source its facts come from, beside the guide. Where launch-week sources say no unlock method is confirmed yet, the guide says so instead of inventing one.

      How the tracker works

      • Tick a trophy to mark it earned. Progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) and survives reloads.
      • Export progress downloads your checklist as a JSON file; Import progress restores it.
      • Hide story spoilers masks the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.
      • Filters split the list by kind: story, missable, collectibles, activities, online.
      • The app works fully offline: it loads only its own files and makes no network request. Source links open only when you follow them.
